Link Loot Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Link Loot is a lead discovery tool for local SEO professionals. This policy explains what information the service collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By creating an account and using Link Loot, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you register, we store your name and email address. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us so we can create and secure your account.
  • Your leads. The business leads you find and save inside your account, including business names, websites and publicly listed contact details such as email addresses, phone numbers and social profiles.
  • Basic technical information. Standard information needed to operate and secure the service, such as sign in events and error logs.

How we use information

  • To create and sign you in to your private workspace.
  • To run your searches, save your leads under your account and generate your exports.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account when necessary.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Your account data and your saved leads are private to your account and are not shown to other users. We rely on a small number of service providers to run Link Loot, including Supabase for authentication and data storage and Streamlit for application hosting. These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

Business contact details

Link Loot collects contact details that businesses publish on their own websites and public profiles, so that our users can reach out about SEO services. If you represent a business and want your details removed from our results, contact us and we will remove them.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and leads while your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by emailing barkatmirani05@gmail.com, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Cookies

Link Loot uses only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and to operate the service. We do not use advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update this page and note the new effective date. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to barkatmirani05@gmail.com. You can return to the app at Link Loot.
